# SULTARO — your farms, your choices

SULTARO gives you a simple place to choose the activities and priorities for your farms.

## My farms

See the castles assigned to you, their levels, kingdoms, player IDs and available resources. Select **Farm settings** to adjust an individual farm. If no farms appear, share your Telegram ID with support.

## Activities

Choose from 21 activities, grouped by castles, resources, army, heroes, alliance and events. Save your choices when you are finished. Castle upgrades and resource building upgrades are separate choices: selecting one turns the other off.

## Farm settings

- **Gathering:** choose the resource for each of six marches. Only available marches are used.
- **Fountain:** choose which resource to receive.
- **Research:** choose a research field.
- **Heroes:** choose which heroes receive experience books. Recruitment, stars and skills still apply to available heroes.
- **Other choices:** choose the castle level to reach, speedups for grain carts, merchant purchases and refreshes, and your preferred pet. These choices apply to all your farms.

The language menu offers 16 languages. Your selection is remembered in Telegram. Arabic uses a right-to-left layout.

Need help? Visit [SULTARO on Telegram](https://t.me/sultaro).

## Preview

The optional `?preview=1` view uses clearly marked fictional farms. It cannot change your choices or perform game actions. Add `&lang=ar`, for example, to view the Arabic design.
